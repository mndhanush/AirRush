Weekly Schedule

Little Leapers: Safe Jumping for Toddlers.

We believe you are never too young to start defying gravity. Every Tuesday and Thursday morning, the park is exclusively reserved for children under 5 and their parents.

Enjoy a completely safe, gentle environment featuring kid-friendly music, sensory soft-play toys, and interactive games lead by our specially trained staff.

Every Tuesday & Thursday | 9:00 AM - 11:00 AM

Kid-Friendly Audio

Soft volume music and nursery rhymes.

Exclusive Access

No big kids allowed. Just toddlers and parents.

Sensory Play

Foam pits and soft-play obstacle blocks.

Weekend Exclusive

Neon Night Flight: Glow In The Dark.

When the weekend hits, the lights go down and the blacklights turn on. Our Neon Night Flight transforms the entire facility into a glowing, energetic club environment designed purely for jumping.

The Atmosphere

Immersive laser light shows across the entire freestyle arena synchronized to high-energy mixes from our live weekend DJ.

The Rules

Ages 13 and up only. White or neon clothing is highly recommended to glow under our heavy UV blacklights.

Every Saturday | 8:00 PM - 11:00 PM
