Our Commitment

Safety First.
Always.

We've engineered every inch of AirRush to provide a thrilling yet entirely secure environment. Learn about our rules, sanitation standards, and sign your digital waiver.

Park Guidelines

The Golden Rules

To keep everyone smiling and injury-free, all jumpers must adhere to these strict park regulations at all times.

Grip Socks Required

Bare feet and regular socks are strictly prohibited. Official AirRush grip socks must be worn at all times to prevent slipping.

One Per Trampoline

Only one jumper is allowed on a single trampoline square at a time. No sharing squares to prevent collisions.

No Double Bouncing

Attempting to transfer energy to another jumper to make them bounce higher is dangerous and strictly enforced against.

Empty Your Pockets

Phones, keys, coins, and sharp objects must be secured in a locker. Belt buckles and loose jewelry are also not permitted.

Know Your Limits

Do not attempt flips, tricks, or maneuvers beyond your current skill level. Always land on both feet.

Listen to Staff

Our Jump Guards are there for your protection. Their instructions must be followed immediately and without question.

Legal Requirement

No Waiver,
No Jumping.

To participate in any activity at AirRush, all guests must have a signed liability waiver on file.

If the jumper is under 18 years of age, the waiver must be signed by a parent or legal guardian. Waivers are valid for one year from the date of signing.

Health Standards

Pristine & Sanitized

We maintain hospital-grade cleanliness standards to ensure our park is a healthy environment for physical activity.

Nightly Deep Cleans

Every night, our entire facility—from trampoline mats to foam cubes—is treated with an antimicrobial fogging system.

Sanitizing Stations

Touchless hand sanitizer dispensers are located at the entrance of every single attraction zone and party room.

Advanced Air Filtration

Our HVAC system uses advanced HEPA filtration to constantly cycle in fresh air and remove airborne particulates.

Trained to Protect

The Jump Guard Team

Our Staff

Vigilance & Care.

AirRush "Jump Guards" aren't just referees; they are highly trained safety professionals stationed strategically throughout the park to ensure rules are followed and assistance is immediately available.

CPR & First Aid Certified

Every manager and head guard on duty holds active certifications in First Aid, CPR, and AED usage.

Active Zone Monitoring

Guards rotate zones every 30 minutes to prevent fatigue, ensuring sharp focus and constant visual coverage of all jumpers.

Strict Rule Enforcement

Our staff is empowered to issue warnings, sit jumpers out, or eject individuals who repeatedly compromise park safety.
