The AirRush Story

Defying Gravity
Since 2018.

We didn't just build a trampoline park. We built a gravity-defying community where people of all ages can soar, flip, and create unforgettable memories.

Our Mission

Elevating the Standard of Fun.

It started with a simple idea: screens are keeping us grounded. We wanted to create a massive indoor playground that physically lifts people up, gets their hearts racing, and forces them to smile.

Today, AirRush is the premier indoor action park destination. With over 40,000 square feet of innovative attractions, we are dedicated to providing a safe, thrilling, and inclusive environment for families, athletes, and thrill-seekers alike.

The Goal

To be the happiest, most energetic place in the city.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

We built AirRush on a foundation of principles that guide every jump, flip, and party we host.

Uncompromising Safety

Fun means nothing if you aren't safe. Our equipment is industry-leading, our padding is over-engineered, and our jump guards are rigorously trained to keep the environment secure.

Inclusive Community

From toddlers finding their footing to parkour athletes perfecting their craft, AirRush is designed for everyone. We foster a supportive, bully-free zone where everyone belongs.

Constant Innovation

We never settle. We are constantly upgrading our park with interactive tech, new ninja obstacles, and fresh event concepts to ensure every visit feels like a brand new adventure.
