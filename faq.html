General Information

While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend booking your jump session online in advance. Our park frequently reaches capacity, especially on weekends, holidays, and school breaks. Booking online guarantees your spot and saves time at check-in.

AirRush is for all ages! If you can walk, you can jump. We have a dedicated "Little Leapers" zone strictly for children under 46 inches tall to ensure they can play safely without older jumpers. However, anyone under 13 must be accompanied by an adult in the facility.

We recommend comfortable athletic clothing that allows you to move freely. Please avoid clothing with sharp objects like studs, large buckles, or zippers that could damage the trampolines. Empty your pockets before jumping. AirRush grip socks are required.

No, spectators are completely free! Parents and non-jumping guests are welcome to relax in our cafe area, use our free Wi-Fi, and watch from the viewing platforms. You only need a ticket and wristband if you are entering the active jump zones.

Safety & Rules

Yes, absolutely. A valid liability waiver is legally required for every person entering the jump zones. Jumpers under the age of 18 must have their waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian. Waivers stay on file for exactly one year from the date of signing.

Safety and hygiene. Regular socks are extremely slippery on trampoline material, and bare feet are not permitted for sanitary reasons. AirRush grip socks have custom silicone traction strips on the bottom to help you stick your landings. They cost $3.50 and are yours to keep and reuse on future visits.

  • Always follow the Jump Guards' instructions.
  • One person per trampoline square.
  • No double bouncing.
  • Do not jump if you are pregnant or have health limitations.
  • Always land on both feet or your seated bottom.
  • No running across the pads.

Parties & Events

General admission guests cannot bring outside food or drink. However, if you have booked an official Birthday Party Package and have a reserved party room, you are permitted to bring your own birthday cake, cupcakes, or sealed ice cream.

We recommend that the host and all guests arrive 15 to 20 minutes prior to your scheduled jump time. This allows enough time to check waivers, hand out grip socks, apply wristbands, and watch the brief safety video without cutting into your jump time.

A 50% deposit is required at the time of booking. This deposit is fully refundable up to 14 days prior to your event. Cancellations within 14 days of the event forfeit the deposit, but you may reschedule your party to a new date within 6 months at no additional penalty.
